Input

Pucks. Drag to move. right-drag or wheel over a puck rotates it, [ ] nudge 5°. P drops a puck at the cursor, ⌫ removes one. Drag a puck off the edge to lift it off the table.
Contacts. Drag empty space for a contact that lasts while the button is down. 1…9 pin a lasting contact at the cursor, press again to lift it. esc clears all contacts. A real touchscreen gives you true simultaneous fingers.
Pinch pair makes a drag emit two contacts; wheel changes the spread, shift+wheel rotates the pair.
