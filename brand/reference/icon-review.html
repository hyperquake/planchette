Product identity, round one

Planchette

One object, several hands, one flat surface. The planchette is the only thing in the world designed to be touched by more than one person at a time, which makes it the right mark for a tool that simulates a multi touch table.

Specification

Palette

  • White #ffffff ground
  • Black #000000 body
  • Poppy #f04e23, one accent, the lens only

Construction

  • Convex hull of three circles: a 32px radius point, two 156px radius base corners
  • Flat tangent across the base, so it sits like a real planchette
  • Lens knocked out with an even odd fill, never a mask
  • Mark occupies 640 x 664 within a 1024 tile

Brand notes

  • No parallelogram in the mark, so the no rounding rule stays intact
  • Subtle radius applies only to the tile and to images
  • Squint test: white, black, one pop of Poppy
